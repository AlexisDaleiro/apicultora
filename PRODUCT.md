## Platform

web

## Stack

Brief-delegated: Next.js 16 (App Router) + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS 4, static export (`output: 'export'`). Chosen because the repository was empty and the brief names this stack as the default for a new project. No backend, database, auth or analytics.

## Users

- A person who has just found a bee colony or a wasp nest at home, at work or on a property. Possibly worried, on a phone held with one hand, and often unsure whether the insects are bees or wasps. Job: find out whether this company can help and contact it.
- People who manage a company, a property or a building, evaluating whether the company is a serious specialist.

## Product Purpose

Landing page for a specialist service: removal of bee colonies (*Apis mellifera*) and resolution of situations with wasps. Success = the visitor starts a WhatsApp or phone enquiry. Secondary: understanding where relocated bee colonies go.

This build is a sales demo for a redesign proposal, not the published site.

## Positioning

Client's own words (priority source): «Empresa especializada en el retiro, reubicación y resolución en casos de colmenas de abejas (Apis mellifera) y avispas. Para los casos de reubicación de colonias rústicas se llevarán a apiarios trazabilizados en diferentes predios del sur de nuestro país».

Distinctive, true claim: rustic bee colonies that are relocated go to traceable apiaries ("apiarios trazabilizados") on different properties in southern Uruguay. This applies to bees only, never to wasps, and not every colony is necessarily relocated.

## Operating Context

## Capabilities and Constraints

- In scope: removal of bee colonies, relocation of rustic bee colonies when it applies, situations with wasp nests.
- Out of scope: honey, products, courses, subscriptions, apiary visits, any other service.
- Service area: unconfirmed. Use «Consultanos por tu localidad». Southern Uruguay is only the destination of relocated colonies.
- Undecided / must not be invented: prices, free quotes, 24/7, response times, guarantees, chemical-free claims, construction repair, aftercare, certifications, registries, insurance.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: «Colmenas del Río». No vector logo exists; any wordmark in the demo is a proposal, not an official seal.
- Voice: Rioplatense Spanish (contanos, consultanos, podés), professional and direct, no humour about stressful situations, no agency slogans.
- The previous demo (https://colmenasdelrio-kappa.vercel.app/: petrol green, yellow accents, honeycomb pattern, hexagon-cropped photo, cartoon bees) was rejected and is an anti-reference.

## Product Principles

1. Contact first: phone and WhatsApp reachable without reading, scrolling far or filling anything in.
2. "I don't know what insect it is" is a valid way in.
3. Every claim traceable to the client's words; bees and wasps never conflated.
4. Specialist trade, not a honey shop, eco campaign or pest-control flyer.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target: applicable WCAG 2.2 AA criteria, verified manually on the main flows. Project rule: 44 × 44 px touch targets. Must work at 320 px width, 200 % zoom, without JavaScript (content and contacts) and with reduced motion.
