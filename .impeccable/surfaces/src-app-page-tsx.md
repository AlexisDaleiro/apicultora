## Scope

Landing page `/` — Persuade. Audience: person who found a colony or nest (phone, one hand, worried, unsure of the insect) and property/company managers. Action: start WhatsApp or phone enquiry. Proof: one real photo + the client's own relocation statement. Constraints: see PRODUCT.md; brief-pinned palette, type and section sequence.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A specialist trade presented like a documentary field publication: type sets the page, one real photograph is treated as a document, and the relocation of bees is explained by one continuous drawn plate (three panels on a shared ground and route line on desktop, stacked on mobile). Refuses the category default (dark hero, honey yellow, honeycomb, icon cards).

OWN-WORLD: Paper #F2F0E8 ground, ink #202723 text, deep olive #3D4A36 for the primary action and the relocation spread, oxide #A1442F only for editorial marks and focus. Archivo (semi-condensed heavy display, normal-width text), Source Serif 4 only inside the relocation section (statement, step numerals, figure caption) plus the italic species name *Apis mellifera* as a specimen label; every other caption is Archivo, per the brief. Hairline rules, folio lines, rectangular photo, 4 px corners, no shadows.

STORY: The visitor reads what the company does, sees they can ask without knowing the insect, taps WhatsApp or call; scrolling explains bees vs wasps, then where relocated bees go, then an optional message helper.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: header (wordmark, 3 links, WhatsApp button); folio rule; H1 across 8 columns at up to 112 px; right column with support text, WhatsApp + call actions and the "no necesitás saber" note, top-aligned with the H1 (foot alignment left a hole at 1024–1280); below, the photo offset into columns 5–10 with its caption hanging in columns 1–3. Mobile: wordmark + WhatsApp, inline nav row, H1 ~44 px, actions stacked, then photo.

FORM: Editorial documentary publication, brief-pinned (position 1 of 1). seed: none (brief-pinned; new-work.md: "A session that reaches directions or comps without concept-seed having run (a direction the user or brief pinned) reads the mode's file instead", so no roll ran). Signature interaction: the relocation plate's line work draws itself once as it enters view (CSS scroll-driven, static when unsupported or reduced).
